        How to play

        1. Hit play — you only hear a short clip of the song.
        2. Start typing a title and pick it from the list. Picking submits the guess right away.
        3. A wrong guess or a Skip unlocks a longer clip.
        4. You get 5 tries per stage. The sooner you get it, the better.

        Daily Challenge

        Guess one song in five tries. Solve consecutive daily challenges to build your win streak. A missed day or an incorrect final result resets it. Progress is saved in this browser. A new song arrives at 7:00 AM in your local time.

        Classic · Five stages

        One run is 5 stages, each harder than the last: Easy → Medium → Hard → Expert → Impossible. There is one song per stage, and the songs get more obscure as you progress. Every stage uses the same clip lengths: 0.1s → 0.5s → 2s → 8s → 15s. Each wrong guess or Skip uses one try; after the fifth, the song is revealed.

        Missing a stage does not end the run — you carry on to the next one all the way to Impossible. Your score is how many stages you nailed, out of 5.
