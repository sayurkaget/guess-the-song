          About the game

          TuneUp brings free guess the song games together right in your browser. Put your ear to the test with 0.1s–15s audio clips in Daily, Classic (33,500+ tracks), Artist, and Spotify, Apple Music & YouTube Playlist modes, compare hit songs in Music Duel, or deduce 530 global artists from 6 clues in Artist Detective.

          Play for free, anytime you want — no sign-up required.

          Good to know

          Do I need an account?

          No sign-up is needed. Open the game on your phone or computer and pick a mode, or paste any public Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube playlist or album link to play your own tracks.

          What can I replay?

          Classic, Custom Artist, Spotify, Apple Music & YouTube Playlists, Music Duel, Artist Detective Endless, and all 5 mini-games (Cover Pixel, MV Spot, HairSpot, Fact or Cap, Lyric Guess) can be played as often as you like. The Daily Challenge resets once per day.

          When is the next Daily?

          At 7:00 AM in your device's local time. Consecutive Daily wins build your streak; a missed day or a loss resets it.

          Can I challenge a friend?

          Yes. After any song, 5-stage run, Music Duel, or Artist Detective round, tap Challenge your friend to copy a spoiler-free link with the exact same tracks or mystery artist.

          Where is my progress saved?

          Your Daily progress, streaks, stats, and settings are saved in this browser. Open Settings → Backup & transfer to copy a backup code and restore your progress on another browser or device.
