        About the game

        TuneUp brings quick music games together right in your browser. Put your ear to the test with 0.1s–15s audio clips in Daily, Classic, and Custom Artist modes, compare hit songs in Music Duel, or deduce 250 global artists from 6 clues in Artist Detective.

        Play for free, anytime you want.

        Good to know

        Do I need an account?

        No sign-up is needed. Open the game on your phone or computer and pick a mode.

        What can I replay?

        Classic, Custom Artist, Music Duel, and Artist Detective can be played as often as you like. The Daily song is available once per day, with five attempts.

        When is the next Daily?

        At 7:00 AM in your device's local time. Consecutive Daily wins build your streak; a missed day or a loss resets it.

        Can I challenge a friend?

        Yes. After any song, 5-stage run, Music Duel, or Artist Detective round, tap Challenge your friend to copy a spoiler-free link with the exact same tracks or mystery artist.

        Where is my progress saved?

        Your Daily progress and stats are saved in this browser. They do not automatically transfer to another browser or device.
