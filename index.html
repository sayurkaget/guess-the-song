    About the game

    Name The Track is a music guessing game built around short audio clips. Put your music memory to the test with a five-stage Classic run, a single Daily song, or a Custom game featuring an artist you choose.

    Play for free, right in your browser.

    Good to know

    Do I need an account?

    No sign-up is needed. Open the game on your phone or computer and pick a mode.

    What can I replay?

    Classic and Custom games can be played as often as you like. The Daily song is available once per day, with five attempts.

    When is the next Daily?

    At 7:00 AM in your device's local time. Consecutive Daily wins build your streak; a missed day or a loss resets it.

    Where is my progress saved?

    Your Daily progress and stats are saved in this browser. They do not automatically transfer to another browser or device.
